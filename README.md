# Audits

Independent, read-only reviews by [AlienPOWER@Hour](https://github.com/AlienPOWERHour). Each write-up cites the public records it used, separates what was verified from what remains uncertain, and links the author's responses where they exist.

These reviews are not production certifications, and they do not claim results for systems or rounds that were outside the stated scope.

## Method

Reviews are human-directed and supported by Guardian (private / in development) and AI collaborators. Typical constraints:

- Read-only use of public or sanitized information
- No request for credentials, wallet keys, or access to funds
- No deanonymization of participants
- Findings tied to a named cut, commit, or export digest
- A clear record of what was left open
